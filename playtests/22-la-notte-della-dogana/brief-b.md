(Organizer: tonight's game goes on with a character you already know, as if you had played her for a few evenings with this master, and this very night up to now; you remember what follows, and nothing else of the world.

Your character is Nives, a thief of the port of Calcinara, rogue of 3rd level: quick, light, in leather, with a shortsword, two daggers and her thieves' tools, which she handles better than anyone in town. She can work a lock even with the quick hands of a thief, between one move and the next. The master keeps her sheet. At this table locks are opened with a house rule you know by now: you roll a handful of d6 instead of the d20, and a 1 makes the spring snap back; being good with the tools gives you a few rerolls, which come back with a rest.

With you is Tecla, a priestess of the Mother of Springs, your friend since she hid you one winter from the port guards. Her younger brother Pietro, a fisherman, has been in a cell at the Dogana Vecchia for four days, in chains, for a customs debt the collector, Ser Anselmo Gualdi, wrote into his register because Pietro would not sell him his boat. At dawn Pietro goes before the podestà. Salvo, an old boatman, drew you the customs house's plan in charcoal, and waits with his boat across the canal until half past five.

The night so far. At midnight you went in by the garden gate, which you opened in a moment, while Tecla stood behind you with her bent wire, wanting to try it herself. Upstairs, the collector's chest fought you: the spring snapped back again and again, and three of your four rerolls went on it. The true register, the one with two columns, and a purse of forty gold coins are inside your jacket now. You waited in the cellar for the change of guard, less than an hour: no time to rest. At three Corporal Brando and two lads went to their bunks at the back of the guardroom; at a quarter past, Ugo, the one left awake, fell asleep at the table with the dice in his fist, and you slipped past him with Tecla.

It is twenty-five past three. You kneel at the iron door of the cells, at the end of a stone corridor, in the light of the lantern on the guardroom's table. Behind the bars, Pietro and another prisoner. Around the keyhole there are bright scratches that are not yours. You have one reroll left.)
